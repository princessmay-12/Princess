Event Overview ★

SMCC-CCIS Week 2026 is an annual campus event that highlights student projects, programming skills, innovation, cybersecurity awareness, and collaboration among future IT professionals.


Join us for a week of learning, networking, and fun through competitions, seminars, and exciting technology activities.

Event Info & Contacts

SMCC-CCIS Week 2026 is organized by the College of Computing and Information Sciences.


For inquiries: ccisweek@smccschool.edu


Event Highlights

  • Live coding sessions
  • Industry speakers
  • Project exhibits
  • Hackathon competitions
  • Cybersecurity workshop
